Week 5: 3D Printing

I made a 3D printed ring since the curvs in multiple dimensions is really hard to acheive with a laser cutter.

a link to download a file.

I made 2 circles one 30mm and one 40mm in a sketch then extruded the area inbetween them 7mm. On a seperate axis I used the 3 point arc tool to make an arc on the inside and outside edges of the ring. I then revolved them around the middle of the ring to get a curved inside and outside that makes the ring easy to take on and of.
