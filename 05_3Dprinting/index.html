Week 5: 3D Printing

I made a 3D printed ring since the curvs in multiple dimensions is really hard to acheive with a laser cutter.

a link to download a file.

I made 2 circles one 30mm and one 40mm in a sketch then extruded the area inbetween them 7mm. On a seperate axis I used the 3 point arc tool to make an arc on the inside and outside edges of the ring. I then revolved them around the middle of the ring to get a curved inside and outside that makes the ring easy to take on and of. I then Made the teeth using the arc function and revolved the teeth to make them 3d.

Project bill of matirials.

6 Stepper moters 4 servo moters 1 micro controler 2 PS3 contolers string Laser cut areana frame 3D printed bodys 3D pronted heads 2 springs

I want to make the robots move on a platform that is moved by a soinijgn threaded shaft. I want to layer two of these on top of eachother one for each robot. I want to control each arm with string and a stepper moter in the base that the feet are attached to. I also want the torso of the robots to move in order to allow the robots to stand in a more realistic boxing stance instead of square to eachother.
